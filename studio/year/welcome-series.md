# The five-minute habit we love most

Hi FNAMETAG,

If we could give you one small habit, it would be this: **go outside early in the day, and if you can, put your bare feet on the ground.**

Five minutes. No equipment. No cost.

We're careful not to make health claims. What we can tell you is what many people notice: a few quiet minutes outside before the day's demands begin helps them feel calmer, more awake and more like themselves.

## Try it this week

1. Within an hour or two of waking, step outside.
2. Look at the sky (not at the sun).
3. If there's grass, soil or sand nearby, slip off your shoes for a couple of minutes.
4. Breathe. Notice the temperature, the birds, the season.

## Want help keeping it going?

That's what **the Nest** is for. Its Earth Charge timer tracks your morning sun and earthing time and keeps your streak, and the Sky tab shows the moon, planets and sunrise for where you are.
