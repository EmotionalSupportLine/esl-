Monday's new moon in Leo is a total solar eclipse. Its path crosses Spain, North Africa and the Middle East, with more than six minutes of totality near Luxor, Egypt. It isn't visible from the US, but it's a powerful marker in the year's sky. In astrology, solar eclipses are like a new moon with the volume turned up: big, brave beginnings. Mercury also enters Leo on Monday.
