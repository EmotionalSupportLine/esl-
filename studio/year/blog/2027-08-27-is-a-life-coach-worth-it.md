"Is a life coach worth it? Or am I about to spend a lot of money to have someone tell me what I already know?"

Honest question. You deserve an honest answer, even from people who offer coaching.

Here it is: sometimes coaching is absolutely worth it. Sometimes it isn't, and something smaller or different would serve you better. Let's sort out which is which.

## When is a life coach worth it?

Coaching tends to be worth the money when most of these are true:

- **You have something specific you want to change.** A career move, a business, your relationship with time or money, a big decision.
- **You're ready to act.** Not "someday." You're willing to do things between sessions, including uncomfortable things.
- **You keep getting in your own way.** You know what to do. You just don't do it. A coach helps you see the pattern and break it.
- **You can afford it without adding stress.** If paying for coaching keeps you up at night, it's working against you.
- **You've found someone who fits.** You feel more like yourself after talking to them, not less.
- **You want it for months, not minutes.** Coaching is built for change over time.

When those line up, a good coach can help you do in a year what might take you five on your own.

## When is a life coach not worth it?

Coaching is probably not the right buy when:

- **You mostly need to be heard this week.** That's not a coaching problem. It's a human-connection problem. A single conversation with a calm person can do a lot, for a lot less. (See [I need someone to talk to](/blog/i-need-someone-to-talk-to).)
- **You're in fight-or-flight most of the time.** Nobody can follow a plan with their nervous system on fire. Settle first.
- **Something is affecting your health or ability to function.** If you've felt hopeless, numb or unable to get through the day for weeks, please start with your doctor or a licensed therapist. That needs real care, and coaching isn't built for it.
- **You're hoping someone will do it for you.** A coach can hold the flashlight. You still have to walk.
- **Someone is promising guaranteed results.** No honest coach can guarantee your outcome. If they do, that's your sign.
- **Money is very tight.** Start with free and low-cost support. Coaching will still be there.

## Try this: the worth-it test

Answer each one yes or no. Be honest. Nobody's watching.

1. Can I name the one thing I want to be different a year from now?
2. Am I willing to do uncomfortable things between sessions?
3. Can I pay for this without losing sleep?
4. Is my body calm enough most days to think and plan?
5. Do I want structure and accountability more than I want to be heard right now?

**Four or five yeses:** coaching is likely worth it for you.
**Two or three:** start smaller, with a call or a membership, and revisit in a few months.
**Zero or one:** coaching probably isn't the right buy yet. Start with support.
