About Tracee

Tracee Sioux is the author of The Year of YES!, a memoir about a year of saying yes to her Soul, and A Life-Changing Soul Handbook. She hosted the Sioux-Volution podcast and has coached people through career changes, relationships, money and the quiet work of trusting themselves.

She uses Human Design as a practical map: what kind of decisions drain you, which ones light you up, and how to tell the difference before you commit.

Questions

Is this therapy?

No. Coaching looks forward: goals, decisions, next steps. If you're dealing with a mental health condition, coaching works best alongside a therapist, not instead of one.

Do I need to know my Human Design?

No. Tracee reads your chart from your birth date, time and place before your first session.

Why a year?

A year is a realistic amount of time to build a dream, a business or a new way of living. People who try to do it in a month usually quit right before it starts working.

What if it isn't working?

At 90 days you and Tracee review how it's going, and either of you can end it then. After the review, you can leave with 60 days' notice. Your founding price stays for as long as you stay.

What if I just need to talk to someone this week?

For one-time support, a 30-minute call with The Emotional Support Line may fit better.
