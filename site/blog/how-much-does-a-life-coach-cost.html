How much does a life coach cost? (And other ways to get support)

Life coaches typically charge about $75 to $300 or more per session, and packages often run into the thousands. Here's what you get at each price, when coaching is worth it, and lower-cost ways to get support.

Life coaching prices are all over the place, which makes it hard to know what's fair. Here's a plain guide.

How much does a life coach cost per session?

Industry guides put most life coaches somewhere in these ranges (Noomii, 2026):

  • Newer coaches: about $75 to $150 a session
  • Experienced coaches: about $150 to $300 an hour
  • Specialized or well-known coaches: $300 to $500 a session, and executive coaches can charge far more

Many coaches sell packages instead, and three-month packages often cost a few thousand dollars.

What affects the price?

Experience, specialty, how much access you get between sessions, and whether it's one-on-one or group. Higher price doesn't always mean a better fit. The best coach is the one you'll actually be honest with.

Is a life coach worth it?

Coaching is worth paying for when you have a clear goal, like a career change, a business or a big life decision, and you want structure and accountability for months. It's less useful when what you need right now is to be heard, or to calm down enough to think.

Cheaper ways to get support

  • A single support call. At The Emotional Support Line, a 30-minute call with a real person is $75, and comes with a free month in the Nest.
  • A membership. The Nest is $19 a month: Year of YES! courses with workbooks, guided meditations, a live group call every month, and $50 calls.
  • Group calls. Being in a room (or a video call) with other people working on the same things is powerful, and costs less than one-on-one.
