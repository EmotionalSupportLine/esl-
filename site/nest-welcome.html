Welcome to the Nest

We're so glad you're here. Here's how to get the most from your membership in five minutes.

1. Add the Nest to your phone

Open theemotionalsupportline.com/app on your phone. On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, tap the menu, then Install app. It opens like an app from then on.

2. Start Earth Charge tomorrow morning

Step outside early, look at the sky (not the sun), and if you can, put your bare feet on the ground. Tap the timer to log your minutes and build a streak. Your tracking stays on your own phone.

3. Check the Sky

The Sky tab shows the moon, planets and sunrise for where you are, calculated from real astronomy. What it means for you is yours to decide.

4. Pick one course

The Library holds the Year of YES! courses and guided meditations. Start with one class a week. Good first picks: Psychic Spring Clean if you feel cluttered, Time Joy-gasm if you feel rushed, Soul-Conomics if money is on your mind.

5. Join the monthly group call

The first Sunday of every month, 11 am to noon Mountain. The link arrives by email the day before. Bring a question or just listen.

6. When you need a person

As a member, support calls are $50 instead of $75. Book from the Nest's Calls tab, any Monday or Tuesday, noon to 7 pm Mountain.

Lost access?

New phone or cleared browser? Open the Nest, tap the account button at the top, then Restore access, and enter the email you used at checkout.

We're not therapy and not a crisis line. If you're in danger or thinking about suicide, call or text 988 (US) or call 911.
