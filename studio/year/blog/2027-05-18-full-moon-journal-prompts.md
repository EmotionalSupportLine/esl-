About every 29.5 days, the moon is full: round, bright and impossible to miss. The next one is the **Flower Moon on Thursday, May 20, 2027**, in Scorpio.

Traditionally, the new moon is for beginnings and the full moon is for completion: noticing what's grown, what's finished and what you're ready to put down. You don't have to believe the moon has power over you to find it a useful monthly pause.

## A simple full moon ritual

1. **Step outside** if the sky is clear, and look at the moon for a minute or two.
2. **Light a candle** or sit somewhere quiet.
3. **Choose three or four prompts** below and write for 10 to 15 minutes.
4. **Close with one sentence** naming what you're releasing.

Some people like to write what they're letting go on a separate piece of paper and safely tear it up or recycle it. Do whatever feels meaningful to you.

## Prompts for looking back

- What has grown in my life since the last new moon?
- What am I proud of this month, even if it's small?
- What surprised me?
- What did I learn about myself?

## Prompts for letting go

- What am I still holding that's no longer mine to carry?
- What story about myself am I ready to stop telling?
- Where am I trying too hard?
- What would I feel if I let this go?
- What do I need to forgive, so it stops steering me?

## Prompts for this full moon in Scorpio

Scorpio is associated with depth, honesty and transformation. These prompts go a little deeper:

- What truth have I been avoiding?
- Where do I want more honesty in my relationships?
- What am I ready to transform, not just tolerate?

## Prompts for looking ahead

- What needs more of my attention over the next two weeks?
- What would make the next new moon feel like a real fresh start?
- How do I want to feel by the end of this cycle?
