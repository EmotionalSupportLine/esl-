Support Call Agreement

Sioux Ink LLC, doing business as The Emotional Support Line · 1905 N Sherman St, Ste 200, PMB 1405, Denver, CO 80203 · Last updated October 2026

Please read this before your call. When you tick "I agree" at checkout, you accept it.

1. If you are in crisis, call 988, not us

We are not a crisis line. If you are thinking about suicide or self-harm, thinking about hurting someone else, or in danger right now, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or 911, or go to the nearest emergency room. If this comes up during a call, your supporter will stop the support conversation, give you 988 and 911, and may end the call. We don't do crisis intervention, safety planning, welfare checks or follow-up.

2. What a call is

A call is up to 30 minutes of non-clinical emotional support with a real person, never AI. It covers one issue. Your supporter listens, reflects, helps you calm down and helps you find a next step. With your OK, they may offer simple calming techniques such as breathing or tapping (EFT). These are self-help tools, not treatment, and you can always say no. Supporters use first names or pen names.

3. What a call isn't

Supporters are not licensed therapists, counselors, doctors or social workers. We don't diagnose, treat, prescribe or give medical, legal or financial advice, and we can't make decisions for you. Human Design and astrology are offered for reflection only. You remain responsible for your own choices and your own safety.

4. You must be 18 or older

and calling from the United States.

5. Respect goes both ways

All feelings are welcome, including tears and anger. The supporter may end the call right away, with no refund, if there is:

  • sexual content or innuendo
  • threats, abuse, hate speech or harassment
  • pressure for free time, personal contact or a personal relationship
  • intoxication that makes the call unsafe
  • recording, screenshots or posting call content

Repeated problems may mean we stop offering you service.

6. Privacy

We don't record calls or keep audio, transcripts or case files. Supporters may share brief, non-clinical notes with each other, such as what helps you calm down, so you don't have to retell your story. We are not covered by HIPAA. We keep what you say private, except where the law requires disclosure or someone is in immediate danger: harm to yourself or others, abuse of a child or vulnerable adult, a credible threat of violence, or a court order.

7. Payment, rescheduling and refunds

You pay when you book. With at least 24 hours' notice you can move your call or get a refund. A call missed without notice, or cut short by a late start or your own connection, counts as used. Once a call starts, it can't be refunded. Unused calls can be refunded within 30 days of purchase, or donated to the Pay It Forward Circle. Please email us before disputing a charge with your bank.

8. Limits

The service is provided as is. To the extent the law allows, our liability is limited to what you paid us in the last 3 months. Colorado law applies.

9. The rest
