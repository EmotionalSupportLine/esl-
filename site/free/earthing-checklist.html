The Earthing Starter Checklist

A simple, no-hype plan for spending a few minutes a day with your bare feet on the earth. Print it, stick it on the fridge and tick off the days.

Read this week's Energy Forecast

Before you start

  • Find a natural surface near you: grass, soil, sand or smooth stone
  • Check it for glass, thorns, sharp sticks and animal mess
  • Skip lawns that were recently sprayed with chemicals
  • If you have diabetes, poor circulation or foot problems, ask your doctor first
  • Leave a pair of slip-on shoes by the door

The 14-day plan

  • Days 1 to 3: two minutes, standing still. Notice the temperature and texture under your feet
  • Days 4 to 7: five minutes. Walk slowly, or sit with your feet on the ground while you drink your coffee
  • Days 8 to 14: ten minutes or more. Pair it with something you already do: a phone call, reading, watching the kids play
  • Try different surfaces: damp grass, garden soil, sand, a sun-warmed rock
  • Wash your feet afterward

Your 14-day tracker

Tick a box each day you get barefoot outside, even for two minutes. Missed a day? Just start again tomorrow.

Cold-weather options

  • One minute on a patch of lawn on sunny afternoons
  • Hands in a pot of garden soil
  • Save longer sessions for warmer days, and skip ice entirely

Research on earthing is still early, so we don't make health claims. Many people simply find that a few minutes barefoot outside helps them feel calmer and more present. The Nest app's Earth Charge can time your sessions and keep your streak on your phone.
