    Not therapy. Not a crisis line.

    This is grounded emotional support and practical guidance for everyday stress, big feelings and hard decisions.

    We don't diagnose, prescribe or provide crisis care. If you're in danger or thinking about suicide, call or text 988 in the US, or your local emergency number.

    Are the calls with AI?

    No. Every call is with a real person.

    Do I need a membership to book a call?

    No. Anyone can book a call. Members pay less per call.

    Is the astrology science?

    The sky data is real astronomy: actual moon phases and planet positions. What it means for you is a prompt for reflection, never a prediction.

    What happens to my data?

    Your habit tracking and settings stay on your own device. We keep your email and your Stripe payment record, nothing else.

    Can I cancel?

    Yes, anytime, using the link in your receipt email. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

    When are calls?

    . We call you at the number you give us. Anyone in the United States is welcome.
