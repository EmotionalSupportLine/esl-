The Support Plan

A monthly plan with support calls included, so you know a real person is there when life gets heavy. Not just when it's an emergency.

Right now you can book single calls ($75, or $50 for Nest members). Some people would rather not decide each time. They just want to know support is part of their month.

So we're shaping a plan like this:

  • Calls included every month with a trained supporter. Real people, never AI.
  • Easy to use: your card on file, pick an open time, and we call you.
  • The same supporter again when you want continuity.
  • Everything in the Nest included: the daily tools, courses, meditations and the monthly group call.
