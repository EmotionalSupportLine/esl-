The Sun moves into Pisces on Thursday. Then Saturday's full Snow Moon in Virgo is also a penumbral lunar eclipse, a subtle shading as the moon passes through the outer edge of Earth's shadow. It closes this eclipse season. Virgo full moons are good for looking at your daily routines and what needs adjusting.
