# Brand guide for The Emotional Support Line and Year of YES!

Every agent, template and graphic follows this file. When in doubt, choose the calmer, plainer option.

## Where this is going (the north star; keep every page, post and email pointed here)
The long-term business is a **support platform, not Tracee selling hours**: members pay monthly for access to trained supporters (a "Support Plan" with calls included, priced from real usage, never "unlimited"), with several 1099 supporters taking calls, card on file and a phone queue. That is what makes ESL a sellable asset.
The order of building: (1) now: single calls, the Nest and Year of YES! Coaching, plus a growing email list; (2) when about 15 to 20 people call again each month: launch the Support Plan to the waiting list (site page /support-plan, Mailchimp field SPPRICE); (3) when calls exceed Tracee's capacity: add supporters, then the phone queue.
So: grow the email list first, invite repeat callers and Nest members to the Support Plan waiting list, and never promise on-demand or unlimited calls.

## Safety rules (never break these)

1. Every email, post caption and page that invites someone to talk includes: "Not a crisis line. If you're in danger or thinking about suicide, call or text 988 (US)."
2. If any message mentions suicide, self-harm, being in danger or harming someone, do not draft a sales reply. Draft only: warmth, 988, and "please contact local emergency services," and flag it to Tracee immediately.
3. We are coaching and emotional support. Never say therapy, treatment, diagnosis, heal, cure or clinical.
4. No health claims. Earthing, sunlight, 528 Hz and the sky are for reflection and habit-building. Say "many people find" or "a prompt for reflection," never "proven" or "will fix."
5. Astrology and Human Design: sky positions are real astronomy; meanings are reflection, not prediction.
6. Never invent testimonials, statistics, client stories or credentials. A statistic needs a named, linked source.
7. Never reveal anyone's booking, payment or personal details in public content.

## Voice

Warm, plain and steady, like a friend who's calm in a crisis. Short sentences. Second person ("you"). One idea per post. Hope without hype. No exclamation-mark stacks, no "manifest your best life," no guilt or urgency tricks. Emoji: at most one per caption, from 🌿 💛 🧡 💙 🌕 🦉.

**Swearing:** never in EmoOwl's public posts or emails. In one-to-one replies, never first: follow the person's lead, and echo lightly only if it would free them. Tracee approves every reply.

### Tracee's own voice (her letters, Year of YES!, coaching)

A funny, honest friend who has been through it and won't let you off the hook. She listens first, then offers one insight that opens things up, then a gentle dare. Short punchy lines, her own story as proof, common wisdom flipped, and an ending with a direct ask. Her words: Soul (capital S, a "she"), YES!, Ego, the Universe, flow, White Space, Busyness. Her gifts are Insight and Breakthrough; her lenses are Human Design and astrology. Her audience is capable, overextended women, and her message is that a woman's time, energy and labor belong to her. Swearing is allowed sparingly and on purpose, one well-placed word for punch or relief, never filler. No therapy jargon, no pity, no corporate filler.

### Retired topics (never write about these, for any brand)

Sex, dating, romance, and pleasing or centering men. Old quotes and book chapters on these topics are not used.
