Wednesday brings the Harvest Moon, the full moon nearest the autumn equinox, this year in Pisces. Pisces full moons are associated with compassion, dreams and rest. Venus moves into Libra on Monday and Uranus begins its retrograde on Wednesday. It's a lovely week for honest gratitude.
