Privacy

Last updated October 2026

The short version

We collect as little as we can. Your habit tracking stays on your own device. We keep your email and your payment record, and nothing else.

What stays on your device

Everything you track in the Nest app (timers, activities, intentions, your location for sun times) is saved only in your browser on your device. We never receive it. If you clear your browser or change phones, use Account, then Download backup, to keep it.

What we keep

  • Email, if you sign up for the Forecast (stored in Mailchimp) or buy a membership or call.
  • Payments, processed by Stripe. We never see your full card number.
  • Bookings, in Google Calendar: your name, email and the time you booked.
  • Birth date, time and place, only if you share them for a call, used only to prepare your Human Design chart and deleted on request.

Calls

Calls are confidential and not recorded. We take brief notes to help with future calls. We would break confidentiality only if someone's life is in immediate danger or the law requires it.

Outside services

The free Human Design chart on our site is made by Neutrino; birth details you enter there go to Neutrino, not to us. The app loads public data from NOAA (space weather) and Open-Meteo (sun times, using the location you choose to share). Fonts load from Google Fonts. We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful; it uses cookies and doesn't receive your name, email or birth details. It is not used inside the member app.

Your choices

Unsubscribe from any email with one click. Email us to see or delete what we hold about you.
