Mercury's second retrograde of 2027 begins **Thursday, June 10** and ends **Sunday, July 4**. It starts in Cancer and slips back into Gemini on **June 26** before turning direct.

## What's happening in the sky

Mercury isn't really moving backward. For about three weeks, the way Earth and Mercury move around the sun makes Mercury *look* like it's drifting backward against the stars. It's real astronomy. What it means is up to you.

In astrology, retrogrades are a time to slow down and look back: review, revise, reconnect. We treat that as a prompt for reflection, not a prediction.

## Mercury in Cancer: home and family

Cancer is associated with home, family, belonging and emotional memory. A Mercury retrograde here is a natural invitation to reflect on:

- Old family patterns and conversations
- Your home: what it feels like to be there, and what needs attention
- Memories and feelings from the past that are surfacing
- How you take care of others, and how you let yourself be cared for

**Reflection prompts:**
- What family conversation have I been putting off, or replaying?
- What does "home" mean to me right now?
- What old feeling keeps coming back, and what is it asking for?

## Mercury back in Gemini: words and connections

From June 26, the retrograde moves back into Gemini, associated with communication, curiosity, siblings and the everyday back-and-forth of conversation.

**Reflection prompts:**
- Is there something I said, or didn't say, that I'd like to revisit?
- Which conversations leave me energized, and which leave me drained?

## Practical ideas for retrograde weeks

- Double-check plans, bookings and important messages
- Back up your phone and computer
- Finish half-done projects before starting new ones
- Give yourself extra time for travel around the July 4 holiday
- Be patient with miscommunications, yours and other people's

## Old feelings coming up?

Retrograde seasons can be a time when old feelings resurface. If something from the past is weighing on you, a support call can help you talk it through. Calls run Mondays and Tuesdays.
