## Whoop! Whoop! & Thanks!

It’s Harvest Time! At Year of YES! we learn to say YES! to our Soul’s natural rhythm, nature’s rhythm, lunar cycles and menstrual cycles. We flow with it because that’s easier and more effective than fighting currents, tides, natural energy levels and night.

Autumn is the time during the natural cycle when we harvest what we planted in the spring and plow under the stuff that we don’t want anymore. Trees release their leaves and go into hibernation. Animals of various kinds hibernate in caves and underground tunnels and some even go into a death-like stasis. The moon gets more play in the Western hemisphere and our bodies and minds slooooow dooooown. When we resist the natural flow of nature and try to keep up our summer pace and our spring enthusiasm we get frustrated and meet roadblocks.

Most of the “produce” of the year has been “produced.” There’s still a few more weeks to wrap up some unfinished business, but essentially, it’s time to assess and reexamine our priorities, values and intentions.

Autumn time is about reflection, releasing, completion and celebration. In this course you will:

- Review your intentions
- Look at your experience
- Assess your year
- Re-assess your original intentions
- Release what’s not serving you
- Complete what’s nagging at you
- Train the Universe with gratitude and celebration

This course can be started and completed at your convenience. However, the sequential order is important. The order in which we attract, release, complete and celebrate follows the natural cycles of life and will Maxcelerate your results.

## Class 1: Reflecting on Intentions

It’s time to look at your January intentions, goals, resolutions, and vows. Often when we make a resolution or intention we get mired in busyness and the minutiae of life and we forget what we originally intended. Sometimes we get all pumped up about a whole new life change, or following that Soul Purpose or getting in shape (for good this time) … and we lose enthusiasm by February.

I usually go astray from my intentions when “life” sets in. The bills need to be paid and there’s not enough left to save or when you realize that a 5:30 pm workout isn’t really reasonable considering your parenting duties or when shit just gets harder than you thought.

So let’s go back and look at those goals and resolutions and intentions. Just to see where we are. This isn’t judgment time. It’s just reflection. No pressure.

It’s critical to create a new pattern of trust between you and your Soul, so you must assess where you’re hitting the mark and where you’re missing it. Your Soul has to be able to count on you. You have to be able to count on your Soul.

Don’t worry, the rest of the course will help you resolve some of the feelings that come up as a result of this assignment.

[assignment] Look at your Dream Board, goal sheets, intentions, journal pages, and resolutions from January.

## Class 2: Reflect on Experience

Now that you’ve reviewed your intentions, let’s look at the reality of your experience.

This is not the time to “judge” your year. We’re only observing and reviewing the events that actually happened.

As you do the assignment, simply pay attention to your feelings, the tension in your body, your interpretation about the events.

Notice whether you experience happiness and gratitude, notice if you think thoughts such as, “oh that was such a beautiful moment/surprise/vacation,” or “that never should have happened,” or “this year was really rough.”

Be gentle. You don’t control the entire Universe, so many events are out of your control (but the reaction and interpretation is still in your control). Sometimes we don’t meet our resolutions and intentions because unforeseeable events shake up our world; your mom dies or you’re in an accident and spend the year rehabilitating.

Remember, we’re not judging. It’s observation only right this second.

## Class 3: Adjust Course

Now we’re ready to make some judgments. Now, I don’t mean “you suck” kind of judgments. I mean “hey, is this what I really want” or “I don’t really want that” judgments.

There are many reasons why we don’t follow through with our intentions and many reasons why the Universe doesn’t manifest certain things for us. Let’s look at a few of the most common ones.

### 1. You might not really want it.

There is a difference between a Soul-Level Intention and a surface intention. Now, I don’t mean to imply that your surface intentions are always bad. I mean to suggest that sometimes you think you want something, maybe because you’re “supposed” to want it or you’ve always wanted it or it sounds like something good, but your Soul doesn’t really want it.

The Soul wants what the Soul wants. You can’t make your Soul want what she doesn’t want.

This can be confusing and annoying. Here’s an example: On my dream board I put an image of the Tough Mudder and made this my fitness goal. I was all about being a “Fierce BadAss” that year. I also put “get softer and more feminine” images on my dream board the same year. Now these two things aren’t diametrically opposed, and certainly you can be feminine and badass—I totally am—but my Soul felt like investing more energy in soft and less energy in fierce. I was still into fitness and kickboxing became a passion, but a few months into the year I just didn’t want to do the Tough Mudder anymore.

### 2. You got hung up on the why and how of it.

You know how spiritual people are saying “know your why” these days? It’s all very trendy. But, I think it’s the wrong way to go.

When you start living from your Soul’s Purpose you stop demanding to know why and how and when you’re going to get “it” and you trust your Soul to guide you where you need to go.

Now, when you’re intention-setting and dream boarding you put the end result. You don’t put the “this is how it’s going to happen.” You have an image of the perfect home, but not necessarily how you’re going to find it or what street it’s on. If you get hung up on the how and why you might get yourself stuck, disallowing the Universe from giving you what you really want.

I knew I was going to publish The Year of YES! I didn’t know how. I just knew that one way or another the Universe was going to present me with an opportunity or I was going to create one for myself and it would happen.

In March at a Hay House conference I doodled something I didn’t want to forget on the palm of my hand. I laughed at what the doodle said—Tracee Books—because I hadn’t intended to write it and it’s funny to write your own name on your own hand. My Soul was trying to get my attention and my focus on the book. Later in the conference after a meditation, I wrote: *I’m going to launch a publishing company.*

Guess what? I created my own publishing company—Sioux Ink—to publish my book. It was the best business decision and it just fell into place.

I didn’t know how. I just knew that if I said YES! along the way, my Soul would get what she wanted.

And she did.

### 3. You have stuff to work out.

Sometimes the deadline you set arrives before you’re ready. When you do releasing work on old ancestral patterns and discard some limiting beliefs, it can change everything you want and thought you wanted. You may have some new exploring to do first.

Does that mean you won’t get it? Of course not. A deadline can make you feel rushed and anxious when you aren’t ready yet. Be grateful that your Soul held it off.

### 4. You got it, but the package is different than you thought.

Look closely at your dream board. You may have received most of what you pictured, just not in the form or from the place you expected. It doesn’t mean it didn’t or hasn’t manifested for you. It just means that it came in a different package than you expected.

## Class 4: Free Pass

You can’t make the Soul want what she doesn’t want. Look at those intentions, goals, Dream Boards and resolutions. Is there stuff that you don’t even want anymore? Stuff you thought you wanted but you didn’t spend any time or money on? Stuff that nags at you and hangs on you and feels burdensome?

I’m giving you a Free Pass. You can just let it go. Get rid of it. Not do it. It’s not flaking out or not following through—it’s making a choice. When you say No to this thing that’s sucking your energy, you have that much energy to spend on something you really want to say YES! to.

I have a manuscript on my computer titled *The Girl Revolution*. It was my Soul Purpose. That book had to be written by me. It could not live in me anymore; my Soul just couldn’t bear it. Aannndd… that fucking book hung on me unpublished for four years as something I should be doing.

I just couldn’t make myself publish the book and I couldn’t figure out why. Finally, in 2013 I gave myself a Free Pass. “This year I’m not publishing that book,” I told myself.

And it felt amazing. It was freeing and liberating and the weight of “shoulds” fell off my shoulders and freed me to start another manuscript, *The Year of YES!*

[assignment] Go through that pile of crap … you know the pile I’m talking about. And ask your Soul if you can let some unfinished, burdensome “stuff” go. These things are usually connected with words like: I really should, one day I’m going to, I really need to finish that, I wish I had time for, why can’t I ever get that done?

## Class 5: Releasing Ceremony

*How can you pick flowers if your hands are full of rocks?*

There are things in your life that you just don’t want to bring into the new year. They might be energy, thoughts, practical items, jobs, habits, emotions, objects, relationships. It can be anything and everything that’s driving you bonkers.

They just aren’t working for you. You’re just so sick of it.

For everything that you release you create White Space, a void and empty space. The Universe loves to fill your empty spaces with better, more awesome stuff. If your life is so full of stuff you don’t want, you have no space for what you’re trying to attract.

[assignment] Get ready to burn paper. You’ll need a lighter, a pen, and some paper. You’ll need something to burn paper in. Make sure it’s safe: a toilet or a metal trash can.

## Class 7: 100 Things!

You’re always manifesting, every hour of every day, but often you’re not paying attention.

I guarantee that you did more than you think you did. You accomplished more of your intentions than you remember. Often you’ve forgotten how much you wanted that patio set, because you got it in March and that particular yearning vanished. But, it’s important to remember and celebrate even our smallest manifestations.

When we acknowledge our wins, we’re training the Universe to give us more amazing things. It’s critical to focus our attention on what we did manifest and then shout YESTHANKYOUMOREPLEASE!!! to the Universe. Otherwise, the Universe believes you’re underwhelmed and keeps trying to adjust. Don’t be so stingy with your re-joy-sing. Joy brings more Joy!

[assignment] Go back to those photos, posts and bank statements. Now write down 100 things you did, accomplished, got over, released, learned, manifested, and en-joyed.

OMG! How did you buy all that cool stuff? How did you have all of those awesome experiences? How did you find time to hang out with all those cool people? You’re so freaking amazing! What a phenomenal year you’ve had!

Celebrate it—without celebration the Universe doesn’t know that this is what you want. More of this! More of this! More of this!!! YES!
