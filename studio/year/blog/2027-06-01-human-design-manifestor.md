Two Human Design types share a name and cause a lot of confusion: **Manifestors** and **Manifesting Generators**. They sound alike, but they're designed quite differently.

## Manifestors: the initiators

Manifestors are a smaller group. In Human Design, they're the only type designed to initiate: to start things on their own impulse without waiting for anything outside them.

- **Strategy:** to inform (before acting)
- **Signature (on track):** peace
- **Not-self theme (warning sign):** anger

**What "informing" means:** Manifestors tend to move fast and independently. People around them can feel blindsided. Informing simply means telling the people affected what you're about to do *before* you do it. Not asking permission. Just letting them know. Many Manifestors find it cuts down dramatically on resistance and conflict.

**Manifestors often:**
- Have bursts of powerful energy, then need rest
- Dislike being controlled or told what to do
- Feel angry when they're slowed down or blocked
- Are happiest with freedom to follow their urges

## Manifesting Generators: the multi-passionate

Manifesting Generators (MGs) are a kind of Generator. They have the Generator's steady Sacral energy, plus a speed and multi-tasking quality.

- **Strategy:** to respond, then inform
- **Signature (on track):** satisfaction
- **Not-self theme (warning sign):** frustration (and sometimes anger)

**MGs often:**
- Have many interests and jump between them
- Move fast and skip steps, then go back and fix them
- Get bored with one-track paths
- Do best when they respond to what shows up, then let people know where they're heading
