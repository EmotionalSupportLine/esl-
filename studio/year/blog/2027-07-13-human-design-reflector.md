**Reflectors** are the rarest of the five Human Design types. If that's your type, you may have spent much of your life feeling different, as if you take on the moods of everyone around you.

In Human Design, that's not a flaw. It's the design.

## The Reflector in a nutshell

Reflectors have all nine centers undefined (open) in their chart. In Human Design terms, that makes them deeply open to, and reflective of, the people and environments around them.

- **Strategy:** wait a lunar cycle (about 28 days) before major decisions
- **Signature (on track):** surprise and delight
- **Not-self theme (warning sign):** disappointment

## Why Reflectors are tied to the moon

Most types have some fixed, consistent energy in their chart. Reflectors don't. Instead, as the moon moves through all 64 gates over roughly 28 days, Reflectors are said to experience a full cycle of different energies.

That's why the strategy is to wait about a lunar month before big decisions. It gives Reflectors time to experience the decision from every angle, and to talk it over with trusted people, before committing.

## Environment is everything

Because Reflectors are so open, where they live, work and spend time matters enormously. A few questions Reflectors often find useful:

- Do I feel like myself in this place?
- Who do I feel most like myself around?
- Which environments leave me drained or confused?

Many Reflectors find that changing their environment changes everything.

## Signs a Reflector is out of alignment

- Chronic **disappointment** in people or life
- Feeling lost or unsure who they are
- Rushing big decisions and regretting them

## Signs a Reflector is on track

- Feeling **surprised and delighted** by life
- Being a clear mirror for the health of a group or community
- Taking time with decisions, and feeling good about them
