What we offer

The Emotional Support Line offers non-clinical emotional support and life coaching by phone or video, EmoOwl's Energy Forecast email, and the Nest membership app. It is not therapy, counseling, medical care or a crisis service. We don't diagnose, treat, prescribe or make decisions for you.

Emergencies

If you are in danger, thinking about suicide or need urgent help, call or text 988 in the US or your local emergency number. Do not use our calls or app for emergencies.

Calls

Calls last up to 30 minutes and cover one issue. You pay when you book. To reschedule or cancel, give at least 24 hours' notice for a full refund or a new time. Missed calls without notice are not refunded.

Year of YES! Coaching

Coaching is a 12-month program, billed monthly, with two hours of sessions each month. At 90 days there is a review, and either you or Tracee can end the program then. After the review, you can end it with 60 days' written notice to careteam@theemotionalsupportline.com. Coaching is not therapy.

The Nest membership

The Nest renews monthly or yearly until you cancel. Cancel anytime from the link in your receipt email; you keep access until the end of the period you paid for. Founding prices stay the same for as long as your membership stays active.

Astrology, Human Design and wellness content

Sky data in the app is calculated from real astronomy. Interpretations, Human Design readings, earthing, sunlight and tone features are offered for reflection and enjoyment, not as medical, psychological or scientific claims. Talk to your doctor about vitamin D, sun exposure and your health.

Age

You must be 18 or older to book a call or buy a membership.

Liability

You are responsible for your own decisions. To the extent the law allows, our liability is limited to the amount you paid us in the last 3 months.
