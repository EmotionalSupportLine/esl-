Where 100 Things came from

When Tracee Sioux had a baby and a preschooler, her then-husband told her she'd been doing "nothing." So she sat down and listed what she'd done that year: keeping two small children alive, starting a business and a blog, substitute teaching, and a mountain of invisible work in between. She came up with well over 100 things. He was unmoved. She felt like a total badass.

The full story is in the workbook.

What's inside
