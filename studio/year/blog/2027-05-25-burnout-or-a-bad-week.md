"I'm so burned out" has become something we say on any rough Wednesday. But a bad week and real burnout are different, and they need different things.

## What a bad week looks like

- It has a clear cause: a deadline, a sick kid, a fight, a busy season
- You're tired, but rest helps
- A good weekend, a night out or a few early nights leaves you feeling more like yourself
- You still care about your work and your life, even if you're frustrated

A bad week passes when the pressure passes.

## What burnout tends to look like

Burnout usually builds slowly, over months of giving more than you get back. Common signs include:

- **Exhaustion that rest doesn't fix.** You sleep, take a weekend, even go on vacation, and come back just as drained.
- **Pulling away.** Feeling numb, cynical or detached from work and people you used to care about.
- **Feeling ineffective.** Like nothing you do makes a difference.
- **Dreading things you used to enjoy.**
- **Short fuse.** Small things set you off.

If several of these sound familiar, and they've been going on for weeks or months, it's more than a bad week.

## What helps a bad week

- Lower expectations for a few days
- Sleep, food, water, fresh air
- Say no to anything optional
- Vent to someone who cares
- Plan something restoring for when it's over

## What helps burnout

Burnout usually needs bigger changes than a long weekend:

**Name what's draining you.** Look honestly at where your energy goes. Work, caregiving, a relationship, a role you've outgrown?

**Change something structural.** Fewer hours, a different role, more help at home, a firmer boundary. Small tweaks often aren't enough.

**Rebuild the basics.** Daily time outside, regular sleep, movement, real breaks. Not as another to-do list, but as recovery.

**Reconnect with what you care about.** Burnout disconnects you from meaning. Small doses of things you love can start bringing it back.

**Get support.** Burnout is hard to climb out of alone.

If you're struggling to function, or burnout is affecting your health, please talk to your doctor too.

## Talk it through

Sometimes you need an outside view to see what's really going on. A support call is 30 minutes with a trained supporter who will help you sort out what's draining you and what one change could help most.
