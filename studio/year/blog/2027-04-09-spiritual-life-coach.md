"I want a spiritual life coach. But I don't want anyone telling me what to believe."

Same.

Maybe you grew up with a lot of rules about God and got tired of them. Maybe you never had religion and still feel like there's more to you than your to-do list. Maybe you just want someone who won't roll their eyes when you say "my Soul knows."

You can have Soul work without dogma. Here's what that looks like.

## What is a spiritual life coach?

A spiritual life coach helps you make real-life changes from the inside out. Instead of only asking "what's the plan?", they also ask "what does your Soul actually want?"

It's still coaching. There are still goals, next steps and follow-through. The difference is the compass. A spiritual coach helps you hear the quiet voice under all the noise, and then helps you act on it in your actual life: your job, your money, your time, your home.

## What does "without dogma" mean?

It means:

- **No required beliefs.** You don't have to believe in a specific god, or any god, or anything you haven't already felt to be true.
- **No guru.** The coach isn't the source of your wisdom. You are.
- **No one right path.** What's sacred to you might look nothing like what's sacred to your neighbor. Good.
- **No fear.** If spirituality is being used to scare you into staying, paying or obeying, that's not spiritual. That's control.

Tracee puts it simply: your Soul (she's a "she") gets the deciding vote. Not the coach. Not the church you grew up in. Not your Ego.

## What's the difference between Soul and Ego?

This is the heart of Soul coaching, and it's not complicated.

Your **Ego** is the part of you that was built to keep you safe and approved of. It cares about what people think. It loves the word "should." It says, "Don't rock the boat."

Your **Soul** is the part of you that knows what you're here for. It's quieter. It feels like relief, or a yes in your body, or tears that come out of nowhere when you finally say the true thing.

The Ego isn't bad. It got you this far. It just shouldn't be driving.

## Why does Soul work matter right now?

Work is changing fast, and a lot of people are wondering where they fit. If your sense of self lives in your job title, every headline about AI can feel like a threat. Your Soul isn't a job title. Tracee believes the Universe put you here, now, for a reason, and that finding your real gifts is the steadiest ground there is.

## What are the red flags with spiritual coaches?

- **"Just raise your vibration" as the answer to real pain.** Some things need to be felt, not floated over.
- **Advice to stop seeing your doctor or stop taking medication.** Never okay. A spiritual life and medical care go together just fine.
- **Guaranteed results.** "Manifest $100K in 90 days" is a sales line, not a spiritual truth.
- **They're the only one who can interpret your path.** You're the authority on your life.
- **Shame wrapped in love-and-light language.** If you leave feeling smaller, trust that.

## Try this: the Soul vs Ego check

Next time you're stuck on a decision, take five minutes.

1. **Draw a line down a page.** Label one side "Ego" and the other "Soul."
2. **On the Ego side, write what you "should" do.** What would make people approve? What feels safe?
3. **On the Soul side, write what you'd do if no one would ever know.** No approval. No disappointment. Just you.
4. **Read each side out loud.** Notice your body. Which one makes your shoulders drop?
5. **Pick one tiny step from the Soul side.** Not the whole leap. One step.

You don't have to burn your life down to follow your Soul. You just have to stop ignoring her.
