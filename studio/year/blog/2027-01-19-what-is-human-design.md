If you've seen people describe themselves as a "Projector" or say they need to "wait to respond," they were talking about Human Design.

It can sound complicated at first. Here's a plain-English introduction: what it is, what your chart shows, and how people use it.

## What Human Design is

Human Design is a system created in 1987 by a man named Ra Uru Hu. It blends ideas from astrology, the Chinese I Ching, the Hindu chakra system and the Kabbalah Tree of Life.

You enter your birth date, time and place, and you get a chart called a **bodygraph**. It's a diagram of nine shapes (called centers) connected by lines (called channels and gates). Some centers are colored in (defined) and some are white (open).

Human Design isn't science, and it doesn't predict your future. Most people use it the way they'd use a personality framework: as a mirror for reflection and a language for understanding themselves.

## The five types

The most famous part of Human Design is your **type**. There are five:

- **Generators** have steady, renewable energy for work they love. Their strategy is *to respond*: to wait for something to show up and notice whether their body says yes.
- **Manifesting Generators** are similar to Generators but faster and often multi-passionate. They also respond, then *inform* others before acting.
- **Projectors** are natural guides who see others clearly. Their strategy is *to wait for the invitation*, especially for big things like work and relationships.
- **Manifestors** are initiators. Their strategy is *to inform* the people affected before they act.
- **Reflectors** are rare, and very sensitive to their environment. Their strategy is to *wait a lunar cycle* (about 28 days) before big decisions.

## Strategy and authority

Two ideas do most of the practical work in Human Design:

- **Strategy** describes how you're designed to engage with life and opportunities, based on your type.
- **Authority** describes how you're designed to make decisions. For some it's a gut response, for some it's riding out an emotional wave, for some it's intuition in the moment.

Many people find that simply knowing their authority helps them stop overthinking and second-guessing.
