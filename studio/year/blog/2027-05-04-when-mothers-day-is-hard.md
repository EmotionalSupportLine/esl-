Mother's Day is Sunday, May 9. For some people, that means brunch, cards and flowers. For many others, it's one of the hardest days of the year.

Maybe your mother has died. Maybe your relationship with her is painful or distant. Maybe you longed to become a mother and haven't. Maybe you lost a child. Maybe you *are* a mother and feel invisible, exhausted or unappreciated.

If any of this is you, you're far from alone. Here's how to get through the day gently.

## All of your feelings are allowed

You can love your mother and be hurt by her. You can miss someone and be relieved. You can be grateful for your children and resent how much is asked of you. Mixed feelings aren't wrong. They're honest.

## Plan the day you need

**Decide in advance.** Do you want to be with people or alone? Busy or quiet? Making a plan, even a small one, can make the day feel less like something that happens *to* you.

**Limit social media.** If the flood of tributes is painful, take the day off from scrolling.

**Make it your own.** Some people honor a mother who has died by visiting a favorite place, cooking her recipe or writing her a letter. Others mark the day by celebrating the mother figures who *did* show up for them: an aunt, a teacher, a friend.

**Do something kind for yourself.** A walk outside, a nap, a favorite meal. You're allowed to mother yourself today.

## If your relationship with your mother is painful

You don't owe anyone a performance of a relationship that doesn't exist. It's okay to send a simple card, or nothing at all. It's okay to protect yourself. It's also okay to feel sad about the mother you needed and didn't have.

## If you're a mother who feels unseen

Tell someone what you'd actually like this year: an hour alone, help with a specific task, a real thank-you. Hoping people guess rarely works.

## You can talk to someone

Support calls are open today until 7 pm Mountain, and again Monday and Tuesday after Mother's Day. If Mother's Day is weighing on you, 30 minutes with a kind, trained supporter can help you plan the day, or simply say out loud what's hard about it.

*If grief or pain ever leads to thoughts of suicide, please call or text 988 (US) right away.*

However you spend Sunday, you're allowed to do it your way.
