Dear Old Friend, Reader, Fan,

So... WTF happened to me?

The truth is, I got sick. Around 2018 my thyroid medication was taken off the market, and I went to sleep for several years. Literally. For more than two years I often slept 20 hours a day.

It was devastating, because my whole business ran on MY time, energy, labor and ability to think. I used to believe I had a personality. Then I lost access to T3, and now I know I'm really just a collection of hormones. Without it I couldn't remember a to-do list existed, let alone stay awake long enough to have a life. Running a business was out of the question.

After YEARS of tangling with our shittastic, absurdly horrendous medical system, I did whatever it took to get my T3 back, including seeing a doctor in Mexico and bringing it home for my own use. I'm finally back to a real level of health.

Here's what I learned while I was gone.

Human Design. If you remember, my whole thing was listening to your Soul and saying YES! to that higher Voice within. Human Design turned out to be a roadmap for how your specific Soul talks to you. The minute I saw my chart and it called me a Heretic/Investigator, it rang true deep down.

It helped me hear my own Soul better. It also taught me to talk to other people's Souls in the language they prefer. I waste far less time and energy chasing what isn't meant for me, and my clients stop chasing squirrels and what culture tells them to want. We're all getting better results. So Human Design is now part of my Year of YES! Coaching.

In-the-moment support. I also started answering an emotional support line, solving problems FAST in 20-minute calls. It taught me to cut through the noise and get to what actually makes a difference.

The Year of YES! was about a dream or a goal, a one-year experiment in transforming your life. That was awesome. It IS awesome.

But those calls showed me how much in-the-moment support matters for getting through the daily slog of modern life. We don't just need dreams and goals. We need touchstones and relationships we can rely on. So many people feel isolated and lonely, because things just don't feel like they did before 2020. I miss those hopeful pre-pandemic years and the naive faith I had in the goodness of the world. I know you do too.

But we can't go back. We're on a new timeline, and it's going to be a wild ride for at least another year or two. I heard one astrologer call 2027 "drunk and on fire." That's the forecast. We're going to need in-the-moment support and touchstones just to keep our shit together, individually and collectively.
