Mercury retrograde has a reputation. Lost emails, crossed wires, old friends reappearing, tech falling apart. It's easy to dread.

Here are the 2027 dates, what's actually happening in the sky, and a calmer way to use these weeks.

Each period lasts about three weeks. Astrologers also talk about a "shadow" of a week or two on either side, when Mercury is moving over the same stretch of sky.

## What retrograde actually means

Mercury doesn't really move backward. A few times a year, the Earth and Mercury line up in their orbits in a way that makes Mercury *appear* to slow down, stop and drift backward against the stars for a few weeks. Then it appears to stop again and carry on forward.

That's real astronomy. What it *means* is up to you. In astrology, Mercury is linked to communication, thinking, travel and technology. Retrograde periods are traditionally a time to slow down, review and redo, rather than launch brand-new things.

We treat it as a prompt for reflection, not a prediction. Nothing bad has to happen.

## A calm way through

**Re- is your friend.** Review, revise, reconnect, rest, reconsider. Retrograde is a good time to finish what's half done.

**Double-check the details.** Read emails twice before sending. Confirm appointments. Back up your phone. That's good advice any time, and retrograde makes a nice reminder.

**Give yourself extra time.** If you're traveling or signing something important, leave a little buffer.

**Notice what comes back.** People, ideas and old feelings tend to resurface. Ask: is there something here to finish, or something to finally let go?

**Don't panic-cancel your life.** Weddings, launches and job starts happen during retrogrades all the time. Prepare well and go ahead.
