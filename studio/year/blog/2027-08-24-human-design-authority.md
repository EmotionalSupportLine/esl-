Of everything in a Human Design chart, many people find the **authority** the most practical. It describes how you're built to make decisions.

If you've ever made a choice that looked right on paper and felt wrong in your body, your authority might explain why.

## Why authority matters

Most of us were taught to make decisions with our minds: pros and cons, logic, what we should do. Human Design suggests the mind is great for analyzing and sharing ideas, but that each person has a more reliable inner decision-maker.

## The main types of authority

**Emotional (Solar Plexus) authority.** The most common. People with emotional authority experience feelings in waves. The advice: don't decide at the high or the low. Wait, sleep on it, and let clarity emerge over time. "There's no truth in the now" is the classic phrase.

**Sacral authority.** For Generators and Manifesting Generators without emotional definition. Decisions come as an immediate gut response, an "uh-huh" or "uh-uh," in the moment. The advice: answer yes-or-no questions and trust the first response.

**Splenic authority.** A quiet, instant intuitive knowing that speaks once, in the moment, and often about safety and wellbeing. The advice: listen to the first hit and don't argue with it.

**Ego (Heart) authority.** Decisions come from what you truly want and are willing to commit to. The advice: ask, "Do I really want this? Will I put my heart into it?"

**Self-Projected authority.** Clarity comes from hearing yourself talk. The advice: talk it through with trusted people and listen to what you say.

**Mental (Environmental) authority.** For some Projectors. Clarity comes from talking with trusted people in the right environment, then sensing what feels right.

**Lunar authority.** For Reflectors. Decisions come over a full lunar cycle, about 28 days.

## Try it this week

Pick a small decision, like what to do Saturday or whether to say yes to an invitation. Use your authority instead of your usual pros-and-cons list, and notice what happens.
