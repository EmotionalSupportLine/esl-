The full moon nearest the autumn equinox is called the **Harvest Moon**. In 2027, it's on **Wednesday, September 15**, in Pisces.

For generations, farmers relied on its light to keep bringing in crops after sunset. Around this time of year, the full moon tends to rise soon after sunset for several nights in a row, giving extra light in the early evening.

## A harvest of what's real

Harvest season is traditionally a time of gratitude. But gratitude can easily slide into forced positivity: "Just be grateful!" when you're actually exhausted, grieving or struggling.

Real gratitude doesn't erase the hard stuff. It sits next to it. You can be grateful for your friends *and* angry about your job. Grateful for your health *and* grieving a loss.

## An honest gratitude practice

On or around the full moon, take 15 minutes with these prompts:

**1. What did I harvest this year?**
Think about what you grew, built or learned since spring, including the things that were hard-won.

**2. What am I grateful for, specifically?**
Not "my family" but "the way my sister called me every Sunday when things were bad." Specific gratitude tends to feel more real.

**3. What was hard, and what did it teach me?**
Let the difficult parts be part of the harvest too.

**4. What am I ready to compost?**
What isn't worth keeping, a habit, a worry, a resentment? Let it break down and feed what comes next.

## Pisces full moon themes

Pisces is associated with compassion, imagination, dreams, spirituality and rest. This full moon invites a softer reflection:

- Where do I need more compassion, for myself or someone else?
- What dream have I set aside?
- Where do I need rest instead of more effort?

## Go outside and look

Step outside just after sunset on the 15th. Watch the moon rise big and golden near the horizon. It's one of the loveliest sights of the year.
