Feeling stuck in life is one of the most common things people tell us. Not in crisis. Not falling apart. Just... not moving. Same job, same arguments, same to-do list, same feeling that there should be more than this.

Here's the good news: stuck almost never means lazy. It usually means one of seven things.

## Why do I feel stuck?

**1. You're overwhelmed.** When everything is urgent, nothing moves. *Try:* settle first. The [10-Minute Human Reset](/free/human-reset) helps.

**2. You're unclear.** You don't know what you want, so every option feels flat. *Try:* ask "If no one would be disappointed, what would I do?"

**3. You're afraid.** You know the next step and it scares you. *Try:* name the smallest version of it you could take this week.

**4. You're under-supported.** You've been figuring it out alone for too long. *Try:* talk it through with someone neutral.

**5. You're out of time.** Every hour belongs to someone else. *Try:* write down yesterday hour by hour and circle one thing to drop.

**6. Money is tight.** Money worries can freeze everything else. *Try:* look at the feeling under the numbers before the numbers themselves.

**7. You're disconnected from yourself.** You've been who everyone needs for so long, you've forgotten who you are. *Try:* one hour this week that belongs only to you.

## How to get unstuck

You don't need to find one grand purpose or fix your whole life today. You need one next step that you can actually take. Movement creates clarity, not the other way around.

Not sure which kind of stuck you are? Take the [2-minute quiz](/quiz). You'll get your result and one thing to try today.

## When stuck turns into something more

If you feel hopeless, can't get out of bed, or have stopped enjoying almost everything for weeks, please talk to your doctor or a licensed therapist. That's more than stuck, and you deserve real care. If you're thinking about suicide, call or text 988 (US).
