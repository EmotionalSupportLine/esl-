Mercury's third and final retrograde of 2027 begins **Thursday, October 7** in Scorpio, slips back into Libra on **October 16**, and turns direct on **Thursday, October 28**.

## What's happening

As always, Mercury isn't really moving backward. For about three weeks, it appears to drift backward against the stars because of how Earth and Mercury move around the sun. Real astronomy. In astrology, retrograde periods are traditionally a time to slow down, review and revisit, which we treat as a prompt for reflection rather than a prediction.

## Mercury in Scorpio: what's beneath the surface

Scorpio is associated with depth, honesty, secrets, trust and transformation. Retrograde here invites reflection on:

- Truths you've been avoiding
- Old feelings or memories resurfacing
- Where trust needs rebuilding, or where it's been broken
- What needs to end so something can transform

**Prompts:**
- What am I not saying out loud?
- What old feeling is coming back, and what does it need?
- What am I ready to stop pretending about?

## Mercury in Libra: relationships and balance

From October 16, Mercury moves back into Libra, associated with relationships, fairness, harmony and decisions.

**Prompts:**
- Which relationship feels out of balance right now?
- Where am I keeping the peace at my own expense?
- Is there a decision I keep going back and forth on?

## Practical retrograde tips

- Reread important messages before sending
- Confirm appointments and travel plans
- Back up your devices
- Finish old projects before starting new ones
- Be gentle with miscommunications. Assume good intent where you can

## Old things coming back?

Many people use retrogrades as a prompt to revisit old conversations and feelings. If something is weighing on you, a support call can help you talk it through.
