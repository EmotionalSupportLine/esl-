What actually happens on a support call

Hi *|FNAME|*,

A lot of people tell themselves their problem isn't "big enough" to talk about. So they carry it alone.

Here's exactly what a call with us looks like, so you know it's not a big, scary thing.

  1. You pay and pick a time. $75 for 30 minutes, or $50 for Nest members. Calls run Mondays and Tuesdays, noon to 7 pm Mountain. There's no intro call or sales pitch.
  2. We call you. A real, trained supporter, never AI.
  3. You talk about one thing. Whatever's weighing on you most.
  4. We listen and reflect. We'll ask questions that help you hear yourself and figure out how you want to decide.
  5. You leave with a next step. One clear, doable thing.

That's it. Many people simply want a calm, neutral person to help them think. That's what we're for.
