We start November under a waning moon, the part of the cycle that's made for finishing rather than starting. Mercury and Venus are both still retrograde, so this is a good week to review, tidy up and close open loops before you take on anything new.

## The moon this week

Last quarter on Sunday, with the Moon in Leo.

## In the sky this week

- A quiet week, with no major shifts in the sky.

Retrograde this week: Mercury in Scorpio, Venus in Libra, Saturn in Aries, Uranus in Gemini, Neptune in Aries.
