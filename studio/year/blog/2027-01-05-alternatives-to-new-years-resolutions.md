If you've ever made a big New Year's resolution and watched it fade by February, welcome to the club. It's one of the most universal experiences there is.

It's not a willpower problem. Resolutions are usually built in a way that makes them hard to keep. Here's why, and what to try instead.

## Why resolutions fade

**They're too big.** "Get in shape." "Be less stressed." "Fix my finances." These are directions, not actions.

**They start on the worst possible day.** January is dark, cold, tired and broke. It's a hard month to rebuild your life in.

**They're all or nothing.** Miss three days and it feels like you've failed, so you quit.

**They're about who you think you *should* be.** Goals borrowed from social media or other people's expectations rarely last.

## Alternatives that tend to stick

### 1. Choose a word instead of a goal

Pick one word to guide the year: *rest, brave, simple, outside, enough.* When you're making a decision, ask: what would my word choose?

### 2. Start with one tiny habit

Make it so small it feels almost silly. Two minutes of morning sun. One glass of water before coffee. Writing one sentence about your day. Tiny habits survive bad weeks, and they grow.

### 3. Use the moon as a monthly reset

Instead of one big January promise, set a small intention at each new moon and check in at each full moon. You get a fresh start every month. The next new moon is **January 7, 2027**.

### 4. Make a "less of" list

Sometimes the most powerful change is subtraction. Fewer commitments. Less scrolling before bed. Fewer yeses to things you don't want to do.

### 5. Focus on how you want to feel

Ask: how do I want to feel most days this year? Then work backward to the small actions that create that feeling.

### 6. Expect to slip, and plan for it

Decide now what you'll do when you miss a few days. For example: "I'll just start again the next morning, no drama." Having a plan for slipping keeps one bad week from becoming a lost year.

## Track the small stuff

The Nest app helps you keep tiny daily habits going: morning sun, earthing time and outdoor time, with a streak counter and the day's moon and sky. Everything you log stays on your own phone.

You don't need a new you this year. You need a few small habits that make the current you feel a little more cared for.
